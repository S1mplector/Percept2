# Community Code of Conduct

JVN aims to be a technically rigorous and welcoming project. Participants are expected to communicate with patience, assume good faith while relying on evidence, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Give actionable, specific feedback about work rather than people.
- Accept correction and explain technical disagreement with reproducible evidence.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, names, and identities.
- Keep issue, review, and community discussions relevant to the project.
- Credit ideas and work accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, impersonation, and publishing private information without permission are not accepted. Bad-faith manipulation of reports or review systems is also prohibited.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed in the GitHub profile. Include links, dates, context, and any immediate safety concern. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

This policy applies in project repositories, review systems, documentation, community spaces, and when representing JVN elsewhere.
